Eyewear Knowledge

Vision & Frame Selection Guide

General tips to keep in mind when exploring optical frame choices, maintenance habits, and daily comfort fitting.

Choosing the Right Frame

Selecting a frame shape that contrasts with your facial outline often yields a balanced visual effect. Round faces pair well with angular frames, while rectangular faces complement curved edges.

Comfort & Alignment Fit

Ensure the nose bridge rests evenly without pinching and that the temple arms hold comfortably behind your ears without excessive pressure during long wear.

Sunglasses Selection

When selecting outdoor sunglasses, verify adequate coverage around the ocular region to reduce glare and ensure comfortable protection in bright ambient conditions.

Proper Eyewear Care

Clean lenses regularly using a soft microfiber cloth and dedicated optical cleaning spray. Avoid coarse fabrics or abrasive chemical fluids to protect lens coatings.

Everyday Frame Material

Lightweight acetate and corrosion-resistant metal alloys provide varied benefits in weight, flexibility, and color finish depending on daily routine demands.

In-Person Frame Fitting

Testing frames directly on your face helps verify bridge width, temple length, and visual proportion better than virtual measurements alone.

Important Note: The information provided in this guide is for general educational and frame selection reference only. Khawaja Optics does not provide medical optical diagnosis or clinical lens prescriptions online. Please consult a qualified eye-care professional for medical visual assessments.
